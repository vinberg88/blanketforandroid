# Google Play Store Listing Draft

## App Name

Blanket

## Short Description

Mix relaxing ambient sounds for sleep, focus and calm.

## Full Description

Blanket for Android is a calm ambient sound mixer inspired by the original Blanket desktop app.

Create your own sound environment by combining rain, storm, wind, waves, birds, trains, fire and more. Adjust every sound independently, control the whole mix with master volume, and use the sleep timer when you want the sound to stop automatically.

You can also import your own local audio files and add them to the mixer. Imported sounds stay on your device.

Features:

- Mix multiple ambient sounds at the same time
- Individual volume slider for every sound
- Master volume for the full mix
- Sleep timer with 15, 30 and 60 minute options
- Smooth fade in and fade out
- Purpose-built dark interface
- Import custom sounds with Android's private document picker
- No ads, accounts, analytics or tracking

Blanket is useful for focus, relaxation, masking background noise, or falling asleep in a noisy environment.
